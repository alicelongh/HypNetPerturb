# characterization of Network Perturbations in latent (Hyperbolic) space 

-------------
Contains:
- functions for network embedding in latent geometric (Euclidean/non-Euclidean) space
- functions to simulate connectivity perturbations (disconnection, rewiring...)
- Definition of distance-based scores to localize and quantify the perturbation in latent space, as defined in [1].
- notebooks for the analysis and plot of synthetic perturbations on networks
- notebooks with the example application in two clinical studies involving real network perturbation (Alzheimer's [2], temporal lobe epilepsy surgery [3])

[1] Longhena, A., Guillemaud, M., & Chavez, M. (2024). Detecting local perturbations of networks in a latent hyperbolic embedding space. Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science, 34(6).
[2] Longhena, A., Guillemaud, M., Fallani, F. D. V., Migliaccio, R., & Chavez, M. (2025). Hyperbolic embedding of brain networks detects regions disrupted by neurodegeneration in Alzheimer's disease. Physical Review E, 111(4), 044402.
[3] Guillemaud, M., Longhena, A., Cousyn, L., Frazzini, V., Mathon, B., Navarro, V., & Chavez, M. (2025). Geometric representations of brain networks can predict the surgery outcome in temporal lobe epilepsy. npj Systems Biology and Applications, 11(1), 79.
